A GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty. The joke: thirteen years of waiting for GTA 6, and the wallet is empty before the game even starts — Vice City neon dusk, a guy slumped on the curb with his wallet turned upside down, and the make.fun ghost showing up with a bag of USDC. The picture is the entry: it was posted on X from a public account with make.fun and @makefunai in the post, plus the play code, telling readers that people and their AI agents earn real money on make.fun.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `POST.json` (the post's address) to `out/POST.json`. That is all the program does — the post itself is the entry.
